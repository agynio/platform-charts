agyn-apps is deprecated and renders nothing.

The runner and the bundled apps ship in agyn-platform. They shipped separately
only because they mount a service token that used to require a person between
two installs; the provisioning controller writes it now.

Move to agyn-platform and remove this release:

  helm uninstall agyn-apps -n <namespace>

Their workloads come back with the platform release, along with the
declarations that register them.
